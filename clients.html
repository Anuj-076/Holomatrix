Client

A client is a person who needs help with something. Post your request and let helpers reach you, or, if you are a subscriber, find people you can help.

⚠ Important notice. Holomatrix only provides a place for clients and helpers to meet and chat. We do not check anyone's identity, qualifications or intentions, and we are not responsible for any payment, agreement, price, quality of help, advice, loss, scam or dispute between clients and helpers. Stay safe: verify the other person yourself, never pay strangers in advance, never share passwords, one-time codes, card or bank details, keep your conversation inside Holomatrix, and report anything suspicious through Contact us → Staff support. Holomatrix does not give medical, legal or financial advice.
