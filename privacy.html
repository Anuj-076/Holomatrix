Privacy Policy

What we collect When you create an account we store your email address and your name. If you sign in with Google or LinkedIn, we also receive your basic profile details, such as your profile picture. If you contact us, we store the messages you send. If you post a client request, we store everything you enter in it. Messages between you and a helper or client are stored so both of you can read them. Who can see what A client request is shown to subscribed members while it is open, and you can close or delete it at any time. Your chats are visible only to you and the other person. Do not put private details such as phone numbers, addresses, ID numbers or passwords in a request. How we use it We use this information to create and secure your account, to show you your dashboard, to answer your messages, to run Find a client and Become a client, and to send you emails such as sign-up confirmation, password resets and announcements from the Holomatrix team. You can turn announcements off in your dashboard settings. Services we use This website runs on Cloudflare (hosting), Supabase (accounts and database) and Brevo (email delivery). When you use AI support, your question is sent to AI providers to produce an answer, so do not type private details there. Google and LinkedIn share basic profile details with us only when you choose to sign in with them. Cookies and local storage We store your login session, your theme choice and your cookie choice in your browser. We do not use them for advertising. Your choices You can edit, close or delete your request at any time from the Become a client page, and you can ask us to correct or delete your account and data through the Contact us page.
