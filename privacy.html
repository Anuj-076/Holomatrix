Privacy Policy

What we collect When you create an account we store your email address and your name. If you sign in with Google or LinkedIn, we also receive your basic profile details, such as your profile picture. If you use the support chat, we store the messages you send. How we use it We use this information to create and secure your account, to show you your dashboard, to answer your support messages, and to send you emails such as sign-up confirmation, password resets and announcements from the Holomatrix team. Services we use This website runs on Vercel (hosting), Supabase (accounts and database) and Brevo (email delivery). These providers process your data only to operate the website. Google and LinkedIn share basic profile details with us only when you choose to sign in with them. Cookies and local storage We store your login session, your theme choice and your cookie choice in your browser. We do not use them for advertising. Your choices You can ask us to correct or delete your account and data through the support chat on this website.
