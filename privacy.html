Privacy Policy

What we collect When you create an account we store your email address and your name. If you sign in with Google or LinkedIn, we also receive your basic profile details, such as your profile picture. If you contact us, we store the messages you send. If you create a client profile, we store the details you enter and show them to logged-in members while your profile is visible. How we use it We use this information to create and secure your account, to show you your dashboard, to answer your messages, to run the client directory, and to send you emails such as sign-up confirmation, password resets and announcements from the Holomatrix team. You can turn announcements off in your dashboard settings. Services we use This website runs on Vercel (hosting), Supabase (accounts and database) and Brevo (email delivery). These providers process your data only to operate the website. Google and LinkedIn share basic profile details with us only when you choose to sign in with them. Cookies and local storage We store your login session, your theme choice and your cookie choice in your browser. We do not use them for advertising. Your choices You can edit or delete your client profile at any time from the Client page, and you can ask us to correct or delete your account and data through the Contact us page.
